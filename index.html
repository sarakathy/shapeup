Part 1 of 5

We all have problems.

Things that frustrate us, bother us, or get in our way.

These problems can be prickly, uncomfortable, and even painful.

Dwelling on our problems can get us stuck — but with a tool called reframing, we can turn many of our problems into opportunities for positive change.

Part 2 of 5

What is reframing?

When we reframe, we change the shape of our problem into something we can use — a springboard, a ramp, or a step to something new.

When we change the shape of a problem, we change how we think about it — and that opens the door to creative solutions.

Watch the rocks become shapes — and feel the shift in possibility.
