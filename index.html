Shape Up

A reframing tool — because your problems might just be opportunities in disguise.

We all have problems.

Things that frustrate us, bother us, or get in our way. Problems can be prickly, uncomfortable, and even painful.

Dwelling on problems can get us stuck — but with a tool called reframing, we can turn many problems into opportunities for positive change.

What is reframing?

When we reframe, we change the shape of our problem into something we can use: a springboard, a ramp, or a step to something new.

Instead of a pointy complaint that hurts to hold, we get a smooth, colorful shape — an open question that invites creative solutions.
